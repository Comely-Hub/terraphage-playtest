TERRAPHAGE - playtest build
===========================

Thank you for trying this. It's an early build of a factory game: you
build a drone factory on an asteroid, lift drones up to an orbital
platform, and ship them down to open up the planet below.

Everything is placeholder: art, balance, no sound yet. What I most want
to know is what confused you, what felt slow, and where you got stuck.


RUNNING IT
----------
Unzip the folder anywhere and run Terraphage.exe.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the exe isn't
signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen. Settings (in the menu, or Esc in game)
has the HUD size - turn it down if the screen feels crowded.


WHAT TO TRY
-----------
1. New Game > Normal.
2. Follow the directives at the top right. They walk you through it:
   mine ore, smelt it, make parts, then the three kinds of drones.
3. Build the Item Launcher, lift drones to the platform, and that opens
   Home (Tab switches planets, O goes to orbit).

Sandbox gives you 99 of every drone and every building, if you just
want to play with belts.


CONTROLS (the full list is under Controls in the menu)
--------
  WASD / arrows      pan          mouse wheel    zoom
  1-5                build menu   left click     place / open a building
  R                  rotate       right click    remove (hold to sweep)
  drag               lay a belt   Q              pick the hovered building
  G                  port reach   E              put the tool away
  Ctrl+C / X / V     copy, cut, paste a box       X   delete a box
  T                  game speed   J              fold the directives
  K / L              quicksave / quickload        Esc   menu
  N                  leave a feedback note


SENDING FEEDBACK
----------------
Press N any time in game. Write what happened, pick a mood, press Send.
It saves a folder with your note, a screenshot, your game and a log.

"Open feedback folder" (in the note box or the Esc menu) shows them.
Zip that whole feedback folder and send it to me. Long or short notes
are both great - "I didn't know what to do here" is very useful.

Saves and feedback live in:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\von-neumann\


KNOWN ISSUES
------------
- Placeholder art; some drones and icons are hard to tell apart.
- Balance is a first draft: rates and drone costs will change.
- No sound.
- Saves from this build won't load in later builds.
